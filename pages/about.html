Why I Focus on OT Security

Critical infrastructure is increasingly under threat, yet cybersecurity talent in the OT space remains scarce. Most security professionals come from IT backgrounds and miss the unique challenges of operational environments—where availability matters more than confidentiality, where downtime impacts safety, and where systems can't always be patched.

My maritime research at ENSM highlighted these critical gaps. I saw firsthand how industrial systems think differently, operate differently, and fail differently. This pushed me to build expertise in OT security broadly—understanding not just maritime, but energy systems, manufacturing, transportation, and utilities.

I'm committed to becoming someone who bridges IT and OT security thinking—a rare combination that's desperately needed.
