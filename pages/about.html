PROFILE & BACKGROUND

About Me

Master's student in Cybersecurity & IoT Trust at ESILV, specializing in Maritime Operational Technology (OT) security, penetration testing, endpoint threat detection, and digital forensics.

Maritime & Critical Infrastructure Focus

I am an M2 Cybersecurity & IoT Trust student at ESILV (Paris) with 4+ years of hands-on experience bridging digital forensics, threat response, and operational technology (OT) security across critical national infrastructure sectors.

During my maritime security research at the École Nationale Supérieure Maritime (ENSM), I conducted specialized security assessments on vessel operational technologies, bridge command systems, and navigation software simulation (OpenCPN, Bridge Command). My work focused on identifying navigation attack vectors, GNSS spoofing vulnerability analysis, and mapping maritime compliance to IMO Resolution MSC.428(98) and IACS UR E26/E27 standards.

My professional background spans national civil aviation security consulting, enterprise digital forensics (DFIR), incident response, and hardware penetration testing (Flipper Zero, Wi-Fi Marauder). I specialize in analyzing threat vectors across both IT endpoints and isolated OT environments.
